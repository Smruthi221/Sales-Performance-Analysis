Key Insights

Technology has the highest sales among the three product categories, showing strong customer demand for technology products.
The West region has the highest sales, indicating that this region is an important contributor to the overall business performance.
Sales vary from month to month, showing that customer demand changes throughout the year. This can help the business identify high-demand periods.
Technology contributes strongly to overall profit, making it an important category for business growth.
Sales and profit are generally related, but some products have high sales with relatively low or negative profit. This indicates that sales volume alone does not guarantee profitability.


Business Recommendations

The company should focus more on high-performing categories and regions and maintain sufficient stock during periods of high demand.
The company should review discounts and pricing strategies for products with low or negative profit to improve overall profitability.


Conclusion

The analysis provides useful insights into sales,
profitability and regional/category performance.
